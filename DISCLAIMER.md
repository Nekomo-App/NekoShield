# NekoShield Disclaimer

DISCLAIMER — NekoShield

• NekoShield is experimental alpha software. It is not a finished commercial product and is provided without support guarantees.
• DNS filtering is not anonymity, not a full VPN, and not a security suite. Your ISP and network operator can still observe connections; blocked-name resolution does not hide traffic.
• A "blocked" result only means the domain name did not resolve. Content can remain reachable by IP address, alternate domains, embedded CDNs, or apps using their own resolvers.
• Enabling a VPN-profile app like this one can conflict with other VPN apps — only one can hold the system VPN slot at a time.
• Battery use, connectivity, and behavior vary by device and network. If something breaks, stop protection or remove the app's VPN permission in Android settings.
